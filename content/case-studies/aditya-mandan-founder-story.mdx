## Where it started

Aditya Mandan was born with Spina Bifida Myelomeningocele. Weightlifting started simply — as a way to get fit — and became something much bigger over time.

## Becoming a competitive athlete and a coach

Training consistently, Aditya went from lifting for his own fitness to competing: he is now a **national-level para powerlifter**. Alongside competing, he became a **certified fitness trainer**, turning what he'd learned about training his own body into a way of coaching others.

## Training through Stage 5 CKD

Aditya has been diagnosed with **Stage 5 Chronic Kidney Disease**. He continues to exercise, and continues to train other clients, through it.

Training under a condition this serious isn't presented here as a cure, a treatment, or a claim that exercise reverses kidney disease — it doesn't, and STRENTOR is not a medical provider. It's a fact about how Aditya has chosen to keep showing up: for his own training, and for the clients he coaches.

## Why this is STRENTOR's founding story

STRENTOR exists because of this: physical strength built from nothing, competitive results earned the hard way, and the mental and emotional discipline to keep training and keep coaching through a serious, ongoing health condition. That combination — physical, mental, and emotional strength — is what STRENTOR's coaching is built to help other clients build in themselves.
