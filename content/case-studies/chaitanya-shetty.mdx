## Starting point

Chaitanya Shetty was born with Spina Bifida. He has a stoma in his foot and wears an AFO (ankle-foot orthosis). Before training with STRENTOR, he had doubts about whether strength training — particularly lifting heavier weights — was something his body could handle.

## Coaching approach

Chaitanya trained directly with Aditya Mandan, STRENTOR's founder, who built a program around Chaitanya's specific condition and equipment rather than a generic template.

## Where he is now

Chaitanya was able to resume playing cricket as a pace bowler. He now trains himself regularly, applying the training knowledge Aditya imparted during their work together.
