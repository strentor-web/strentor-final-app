## Starting point

Maninder is a previous STRENTOR client with lower body mobility issues and Type 2 diabetes.

## Coaching approach

Training was built around Maninder's mobility limitations, with the goals of supporting weight loss and helping keep diabetes under control alongside his existing medical care — not as a substitute for it.

## Where he is now

Maninder continues to exercise with these goals — losing weight and managing his diabetes.
