## Starting point

Promila Dsilva is a previous STRENTOR client living with paralysis in one arm.

## Coaching approach

Her training was built around her specific physical capacity — exercise routines individualized to what her body can do, with difficulty increased gradually rather than assumed upfront.

## Where she is now

Promila continues to exercise to stay fit, with a program that adjusts in difficulty and scope as she progresses.
