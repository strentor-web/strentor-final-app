## Adding new topics

Keep new entries specific and non-clinical — "how to," "what to expect," "how to choose," "how to
stay consistent" angles hold up well. Avoid anything that reads as diagnosis, treatment, or a
promised outcome. When in doubt, tie the post back to an existing program page or `/the-strentor-method`
rather than inventing a new claim to support it.
