## Claims NOT found (checked for, absent)

Grepped the full public-facing codebase (`app/`, `components/landing`, `components/programs`) for these patterns from the brief's list of forbidden claim types — none were found:

- "% success rate"
- "spots left" / "limited spots" / "limited seats"
- Any other unqualified "guaranteed" outcome claim outside the disclaimer pages themselves (where guarantees are explicitly *disclaimed*, not made).

This does not cover every page in the app (dashboard, admin, checkout flows were not part of this grep), only the public marketing surface relevant to this brief.
